# Worker Quality Checklist

## Shared Checklist For Every Service

- Worker confirms job scope before starting.
- Consumer can upload before-work photo when useful.
- Worker must upload after-work photo before marking complete.
- Consumer feedback is collected after completion.
- AI photo quality signal is advisory until reviewed with consumer feedback.
- Verified bad work creates score event and support path.
- Repeated verified issues can trigger training, temporary pause, or manual review.

## Maid / Home Helper / Cleaning

- Before: room/kitchen/bathroom state, visible problem areas.
- During: no unsafe chemical use without consent.
- After: cleaned surface/area photo, trash/waste handling, items returned safely.
- Score signals: cleanliness, punctuality, proof upload, complaint/no complaint.

## Electrician

- Before: affected switch, fan, light, socket, appliance, or wiring area.
- During: worker follows safety precautions and avoids risky shortcuts.
- After: repaired/installed item photo and consumer confirmation that power issue is resolved.
- Score signals: safety, correct repair, no repeat issue, no overcharging complaint.

## Plumber

- Before: leak/blockage/tap/bathroom area photo.
- After: fixed area photo, water flow/leak stopped evidence where possible.
- Score signals: leak fixed, area cleaned, no repeat complaint.

## Emergency Service

- Worker accepts only if realistically reachable.
- App should show extra charge clearly before confirmation.
- If worker becomes sick/unavailable, ask guild first, then nearby available worker.

## GigScore Guardrails

- One AI photo signal never decides a harsh penalty alone.
- 1-star rating needs reason and, where possible, proof.
- Worker can appeal disputed quality event.
- Consumer repeated fake complaints should be reviewed before score penalties become final.
