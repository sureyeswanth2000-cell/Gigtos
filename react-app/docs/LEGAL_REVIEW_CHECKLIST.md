# Legal Review Checklist

Use this before public scale and before any aggressive worker acquisition campaign.

## Competitor Experience Proof

- Say "verified previous platform experience" in consumer-facing copy unless counsel approves naming competitors.
- Store only safe proof metadata in normal records: platform name, masked ID/card display, verification status, reviewer, review time, free-access-until.
- Keep uploaded proof documents in protected storage with access audit.

## Refunds And Cancellation

- Finalize exact cancellation windows.
- Define when refund, replacement, wallet credit, or no refund applies.
- Define gateway/payment mismatch process.
- Define cash collection and platform-fee dues terms.

## Worker Safety And SOS

- Define what SOS does and does not guarantee.
- Define escalation path: nearby trusted workers/guild, field operator future, emergency contact, police/medical where appropriate.
- Define false SOS abuse review without automatic harsh punishment.

## Data Privacy

- Privacy policy must cover phone, address, location, photos, identity proof, payment metadata, AI summaries, and support messages.
- Logs must avoid full phone, raw payment tokens, full identity document numbers, and private notes.
- Define deletion/export request process.

## SocioScore And AI

- Score impact must be explainable and appealable.
- AI photo scoring must be advisory until guarded by review logic.
- Manual score override must require reason and audit.

## Subscription

- Free access, paid subscription, refund/credit logic, and override rules must be written plainly.
- Workers should understand that repeated verified quality issues can block refund eligibility.
