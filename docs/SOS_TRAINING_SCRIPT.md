# Worker SOS Training Video Script

Date: 2026-05-24

## Goal

Help workers understand when to use SOS, what happens after tapping it, and how Gigtos protects workers without encouraging false reports.

## Script

You should tap SOS only when you feel unsafe, threatened, trapped, harassed, seriously ill, or unable to continue a job safely.

When SOS is sent, Gigtos creates an emergency support incident. SuperAdmin and the field operator queue receive an alert with your worker account, active booking if available, your reason, and your location only if you allow location sharing.

If you are in immediate physical danger, call local emergency services first. SOS is a platform support alert, not a replacement for police, ambulance, or local emergency help.

After SOS is created, Gigtos support reviews the incident, contacts the worker or consumer where appropriate, checks booking history and evidence, and decides the safest next action. This may include pausing payout, contacting a field operator, blocking future matching, or escalating to SuperAdmin.

False SOS reports are reviewed carefully. Gigtos does not auto-punish without context, but repeated intentional misuse can lead to account review.

For women workers and high-risk jobs, SOS is there to make sure help is visible quickly. Use it early if the situation feels unsafe.
