# Gigtos Policy Baseline

This is the first plain-language baseline for app copy, support decisions, and future legal review. It is not final legal advice.

## Trust Guarantee

Gigtos promise: verified workers, transparent booking price, proof-based completion, and a clear support/replacement path when work goes wrong.

Do not promise impossible guarantees such as instant refunds for every case, fake urgency, or always-available workers.

## Consumer Terms Baseline

- A booking means the consumer is asking a worker to provide a listed service at the agreed location/time.
- The consumer should provide correct address, phone, service details, and safe access to the work location.
- Worker price and Gigtos booking fee must be visible before confirmation.
- Consumer feedback affects SocioScore only through auditable score events.
- A worker who received a verified 1-star rating from the same consumer should not be recommended to that consumer again.

## Cancellation And Refund Baseline

- Before worker acceptance: consumer can cancel without worker penalty.
- After worker acceptance but before travel/work starts: cancellation may affect consumer SocioScore if late or repeated.
- After worker arrives or work starts: support review is required before any refund/waiver.
- Bad work claim requires proof where possible: before/after photos, consumer feedback, worker response, and support review.
- Duplicate payment, failed payment, or gateway mismatch must go through payment reconciliation before final decision.
- Recurring weekly/monthly bookings need their own cancellation window before launch.

## Worker Agreement Baseline

- No job commission from worker earnings.
- Worker subscription is separate from job earnings and starts only after free/trial access ends.
- First 30 launch days are free platform access; founder-managed early costs should be handled manually and reviewed before paid scale.
- Verified previous-platform experience may qualify a worker for one-year free access, subject to safe proof storage and review.
- Workers can use Gigtos alongside other platforms. No exclusivity, no forced target, no pressure to leave another app.
- Completion photo is required before marking a job ready for consumer confirmation.
- Repeated verified 1-star ratings, low SocioScore, fraud, unsafe behavior, or repeated complaints can pause access and refund eligibility.

## Worker Acquisition Promise

Public worker-facing copy should stay simple: no job commission, keep your work earnings, first 30 days free during launch, first year free with verified previous-platform proof, and freedom to use Gigtos alongside other legal work sources.

## SocioScore Policy Baseline

- Score changes must be ledger events, not silent profile edits.
- Every score change needs reason code, visible reason text, old score, delta, new score, status, and timestamp.
- Low ratings and disputes can stay pending until review.
- Workers and consumers should see why score changed and how to recover.
- Copper users should receive recovery guidance, not humiliation.
- Manual score changes require superadmin reason and audit trail.

## Privacy And AI Boundary

- Store reason codes and safe IDs in logs, not private raw data.
- AI may read sanitized summaries, not full phone numbers, payment secrets, raw identity documents, or private notes.
- AI photo scoring can support review, but should not alone punish income or permanently reduce SocioScore.
- Location, notification, analytics, identity verification, marketing, and AI-assisted support need consent records before scale.

## Field Operator Policy Baseline

Field operator is future when funds allow. Until then, superadmin/manual review handles verification and disputes.

Future field operators can verify property/work context, inspect proof, and add notes. They cannot change payment, reveal identity data, override SocioScore permanently, or approve refunds without higher permission.
